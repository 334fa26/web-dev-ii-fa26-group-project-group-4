Custom Order Process

1. Tell us what you need. Complete our custom-order request form.

2. We'll contact you. We'll confirm availability, pricing, and details.

3. We bake. Your order is prepared fresh for your event.

4. Pick it up. Pick up your order at the bakery on the scheduled date.

Custom orders should be requested at least 7 days in advance. Larger or more detailed orders may require additional notice.
