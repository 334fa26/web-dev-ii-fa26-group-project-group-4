A little sweetness. A sense of home.

A local bakery and custome dessert shop in Maryville, Missouri. A place to begin an everyday ritual or dream up something for a celebration.

Our Story

Sweet Clover Bakery opened its doors in 2019 with a small menu, a big love of baking, and a goal of becoming a gathering place for the local community.

Founder Emma Carter grew up baking with her grandmother and eventually turned that childhood tradition into a career. After studying culinary arts and working in several professional kitchens, Emma returned to northwest Missouri to open her own bakery.

What started as a small neighborhood bakery has grown into a local destination for fresh pastries, artisan breads, custom cakes, and seasonal desserts.

Our Philosophy

Bake Fresh. We believe baked goods are best when they're fresh, so we prepare our products in small batches throughout the week.

Keep It Local

Keep It Local. We're proud to be part of the Maryville community and enjoy supporting local organizations, events, and customers.

Celebrate Every Occassion. A birthday, graduation, Friday morning, or Tuesday afternoon can be a reason to celebrate.
