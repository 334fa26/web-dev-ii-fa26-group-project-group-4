Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery?

At this time, Sweet Clover offers bakery pickup. Delivery may be available for select large orders by special arrangement.

Do you accept custom orders?

Yes. We offer custom cakes, cookies, cupcakes, and dessert boxes for a variety of occasions.

How far in advance should I place a custom order?

We recommend placing custom orders at least seven days in advance.

Do you offer gluten-free products?

We offer select gluten-free items. Because our bakery handles wheat and other allergens, we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

How can I place an order?

Visit the bakery, call us, or submit the online custom-order request form.
